How membership works

Kanaverse Club operates as a private social club under South Africa's cannabis legislation permitting private cultivation, possession and use. We're not a retail store — access to products and events is limited to verified members aged 21 and older.

Why members join

Community

Regular meetups, tastings and grower circles at our Rosebank clubhouse — a space to connect with fellow members.

Education

Plain-language guides on responsible use, dosage awareness, and exactly where the law stands in South Africa today.

Curated Products

A rotating, quality-checked range across flower, edibles and wellness — sourced with member feedback in mind.

Kanaverse Club

Kanaverse Club is a private, members-only social club operating under South African legislation permitting private cannabis cultivation, possession and use. Membership is restricted to verified adults aged 21 and older. No products are sold or distributed to non-members, and nothing on this site constitutes a retail offer to the public.
