Built by enthusiasts, run for members.

What started as a small collective of Johannesburg cannabis enthusiasts has grown into a structured community space — built on transparency, education, and respect for the law.

Our story

Kanaverse Club was founded on the back of South Africa's evolving cannabis legislation, which permits private cultivation, possession, and use. Rather than open a retail storefront, our founders chose a private membership model — a legal, structured way for adult members to access quality products and each other.

Since opening, the club has grown from informal meetups into a full clubhouse offering curated products, educational resources, and a regular calendar of member events.

Our mission

To provide a safe, legal, and welcoming environment where adult members can access quality cannabis products, supported by honest education about responsible use and current legislation.

Our vision

To be Johannesburg's most trusted cannabis social club, recognised for community, transparency, and a culture of responsible consumption.

Our legal standing

Kanaverse Club operates as a private members-only club, not a public retailer. This structure lets us operate within South African law governing personal cannabis use — membership review, age verification, and an application process exist precisely to keep us on the right side of that line.
